      The Monographs

      Holmes mentions his monographs but never lets us read them. Here they are written out: his method in each field, reconstructed from how he reasons in the stories, with a reference plate and the scenes where he uses it. Monographs he names are marked by status. Those his reasoning implies, but which he never wrote, are marked “Reconstructed”.

      Plates: the trade and the hand

      The monograph Upon the Influence of a Trade upon the Form of the Hand had plates of slaters, sailors, cork-cutters, compositors, weavers and diamond-polishers. Here are twelve more. Name the trade from the hands.
